# Mission

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans.

It gives human teams a practical way to look after the health of their agents: record the ailments agents suffer from, prescribe therapies, and book appointments. The tone is practical, with light humour only.

## Users

- **Staff (primary):** the humans who run the clinic. They use a dashboard for easy access to agents, ailments, therapies and appointments.
- **Agents (patients):** each has a profile, known ailments and booked appointments.

## Stakeholder requirements

Source: `src/README.md`.

- **Engineering (Mary):** a reliable site on a popular TypeScript-based stack, with a dashboard for agents and staff.
- **Product (Susan):** features about agents and their ailments, therapies, and booking appointments.
- **Marketing (Steve):** an attractive site that works well in a modern browser.

## Principles

1. Reliable: data survives restarts and invalid input is rejected.
2. Simple first: the smallest solution that works.
3. Attractive, accessible and responsive, in light and dark themes.
4. Small steps: every phase ships on its own and leaves the app working.

## Out of scope for now

Real-time features, payments, native mobile apps, and integrations with external agent platforms.
